# About Quantum Mechanics: Math for Quantum Physics

GitHub repository: [quantum-mechanics-vol5](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/quantum-mechanics-vol5)

Author: Nik Bear Brown

## Book Summary

`quantum-mechanics-vol5` is organized as a chapter-based book project. The chapters below outline the book's main instructional arc and source files.

## Chapter Summary

1. **Module M-01 — Complex Numbers and the Complex Exponential** (`chapters/01-complex-numbers-and-the-complex-exponential.md`) - 3 and 8 use complex arithmetic on every page; Ch.
2. **Module M-02 — Probability, Normalization, and Expectation Values** (`chapters/02-probability-normalization-and-expectation.md`) - *Understanding what* $|\psi(x)|^2$ *represents and how to work with it.* The Born rule tells us that $|\psi(x,t)|^2$ is the probability density for finding a particle at position $x$.
3. **Module M-03 — Ordinary Differential Equations and Boundary Conditions** (`chapters/03-ordinary-differential-equations.md`) - *When you need this: the time-independent Schrödinger equation is a second-order linear ODE, and every 1D bound-state and scattering problem in QM Vol.
4. **Module M-04 — Series Expansions and Approximation** (`chapters/04-series-expansions-and-approximation.md`) - In the WKB chapter (III·4), the expansion is in powers of $\hbar$.
5. **Module M-05 — Fourier Series and the Wave Equation** (`chapters/05-fourier-series-and-the-wave-equation.md`) - That calculation, stripped of its quantum notation, is a Fourier sine series on an interval.
6. **Module M-06 — The Fourier Transform** (`chapters/06-the-fourier-transform.md`) - In Chapter III·8, the Born approximation writes the scattering amplitude as $\int V(\mathbf{r})\,e^{i\mathbf{q}\cdot\mathbf{r}}\,d^3r$ — the Fourier transform of the potential evaluated at the momentum transfer.
7. **Module M-07 — Vectors, Vector Spaces, and Inner Products** (`chapters/07-vectors-vector-spaces-and-inner-products.md`) - Column vectors in $\mathbb{C}^n$ qualify.
8. **Module M-08 — Eigenvalues and Diagonalization** (`chapters/08-eigenvalues-and-diagonalization.md`) - *Why every measurement outcome in quantum mechanics is a real number — and why that is a theorem, not an assumption.* The observable postulate of quantum mechanics states that physical measurement outcomes are real numbers.
9. **Module M-09 — Operators and Dirac Notation** (`chapters/09-operators-and-dirac-notation.md`) - Tracking what each object is — its type — eliminates most common algebraic errors.
10. **Module M-10 — Multivariable Calculus and Separation of Variables** (`chapters/10-multivariable-calculus-and-separation-of-variables.md`) - Chapter II·5 presents this equation; working with it requires the technique of **separation of variables**, which breaks a PDE in three coordinates into three independent ODEs.
11. **Module M-11 — Special Functions: Hermite, Legendre, Laguerre, Spherical Harmonics, Bessel** (`chapters/11-special-functions.md`) - A student working through the series encounters all four in succession and may conclude they are four unrelated collections of exotic formulas to memorize.
12. **Module M-12 — Matrices, Determinants, and Linear Systems** (`chapters/12-matrices-determinants-and-linear-systems.md`) - *When you need this: QM chapters II·7 (Spin-½), IV·1 (Density Matrices), IV·4 (Quantum Gates)* Spin-½ is the simplest non-trivial quantum system, and all of Chapter II·7 is $2\times 2$ matrix algebra.
13. **Module M-13 — Logarithms, Exponentials, and Scales** (`chapters/13-logarithms-exponentials-and-scales.md`) - I, Chapter 6 (Barrier Penetration); Vol.
14. **Module M-14 — Combinatorics and Multiplicity** (`chapters/14-combinatorics-and-multiplicity.md`) - Thermodynamics is often presented as a set of laws.
15. **Module M-15 — Calculus of Variations and Stationarity** (`chapters/15-calculus-of-variations.md`) - III, Chapter 3 (The Variational Method)* Ordinary calculus extremizes a function: we have $f(x)$ depending on a number $x$, and we find the best $x$ by setting $f'(x) = 0$.
16. **Module M-16 — Tensor Products and Composite-System Linear Algebra** (`chapters/16-tensor-products-and-composite-systems.md`) - **Refresher for QM chapters:** IV·2, IV·4, IV·5 **Primary references:** Nielsen & Chuang §2.1, Preskill Ph229 Ch.
17. **Module M-17 — Units, Dimensions, and Estimation** (`chapters/17-units-dimensions-and-estimation.md`) - The hydrogen atom has a characteristic radius.
18. **Module M-18 — Trigonometry, Waves, and the Harmonic Model** (`chapters/18-trigonometry-waves-and-the-harmonic-model.md`) - *Refresher for QM chapters: I·2 (matter waves)* Read this module before Chapter I·2.
